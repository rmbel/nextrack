Music data credits

NextTrack is a non-commercial university research prototype. The catalogue combines historical datasets and provider metadata. Dates, genres and listening counts have different coverage across sources; missing information remains unknown.

The public source repository contains a smaller demonstration subset. The optional research datasets below are obtained separately; their presence depends on the installation.

The Echo Nest Taste Profile subset

Installations with the optional listener-ranked addition use the Taste Profile subset of the Million Song Dataset, released in 2011. The research importer counts distinct users per song within its research cohort, excludes documented invalid song-to-track mappings, and selects 20,000 eligible songs. Repeated plays do not add listeners. These are historical cohort rankings, not current worldwide or Spotify charts.

Track titles, artist names and available release years come from the Million Song Dataset. Thierry Bertin-Mahieux, Daniel P. W. Ellis, Brian Whitman and Paul Lamere. The Million Song Dataset. ISMIR 2011. Used under its research and non-commercial terms. No audio or individual listening histories are served by this app.

Genre annotations

Hendrik Schreiber. Improving Genre Annotations for the Million Song Dataset. ISMIR 2015, pp. 241–247. tagtraum CD2 supplies published majority and optional minority genre labels matched by track ID. Research use only, strictly non-commercial. Genre labels do not establish language, mood or energy.

Other catalogue sources

  • Free Music Archive dataset — Michaël Defferrard, Kirell Benzi, Pierre Vandergheynst and Xavier Bresson, 2017. Metadata is adapted under CC BY 4.0. Historical FMA play counts are separate from Taste Profile listeners.
  • MusicBrainz contributors and the MetaBrainz Foundation — supplies saved catalogue metadata and live recording search results. Release years use the earliest release date recorded by MusicBrainz; community tags may be incomplete. Core metadata is CC0; adapted supplementary community tags and genres retain CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 attribution.
  • Apple iTunes Search API metadata — provider identifiers and source links are preserved. Spotify links open listening destinations; Spotify listening history is not used to build this catalogue.
